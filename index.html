If this is you, it may not be your skills. People may not be able to see what you offer, why it matters to them, or why they should trust you with it. More skills, more posts, or another course may not be what's missing.

Most skilled people don't have a visibility problem. They have a translation problem.

Why this happens.

People can't see inside your head. They only see what you show them: your profile, your posts, how you describe what you do, and the proof you share.

From that, they quickly decide: do I understand what this person does? Can they solve my problem? Can I trust them? If the answers are unclear, they move on, even when you're excellent.

Being good isn't the same as being understood.
